Our Story

We believe a will shouldn't cost the earth or take weeks to get.

Wills Assured is on a mission to make proper will writing accessible to every UK adult.

“

The numbers tell the story. The average solicitor charges £300–£500 for a basic will. Two-thirds of UK adults still don’t have one. The connection isn’t coincidental.

We built Wills Assured to close that gap.

Using innovative technology and a guided online experience, we’ve made it possible to create a legally binding will in just 20 minutes — for a fraction of the traditional cost. We’re a startup on a mission, and we’re just getting started.
