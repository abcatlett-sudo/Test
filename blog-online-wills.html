The Online Will Revolution: Why Millions of Brits Are Finally Getting a Will — and You Should Too

For the first time in a generation, the number of UK adults with a valid will is rising. The reason isn't a change in attitude — it's a change in access. Here's what online will writing actually delivers, and why there's never been a better time to act.

💻

Something significant has shifted in the UK's relationship with will writing. For decades, the percentage of adults with a valid will was stuck in the high thirties — stubbornly unmoved by awareness campaigns, news stories about intestacy disasters, and repeated warnings from consumer groups.

Then, between 2024 and 2025, the number rose from 38% to 41% of UK adults. That 3-percentage-point shift represents hundreds of thousands of people who finally acted — people who previously hadn't. And the driving force behind that shift is clear: the rise of accessible, affordable, properly regulated online will writing.

"The proportion of UK adults with a will rose from 38% to 41% in just one year — driven almost entirely by the growth of online will writing services."

— UK Wills & Probate Consumer Research Report 2025

What Online Will Writing Actually Is

First, let's clear up a common misconception. Online will writing is not a chatbot generating a generic template and calling it a legal document. Reputable services — including Wills Assured — use qualified solicitors to draft and review every will. The "online" part refers to how you interact with the process, not the quality of the output.

Instead of sitting in a solicitor's waiting room, you answer a structured questionnaire — in plain English, at your own pace, on any device. The questions cover every decision your will needs to reflect: who your executor is, who your beneficiaries are, what happens to specific assets, your guardianship wishes for children, your funeral preferences, and how to handle your digital estate.

A qualified solicitor then reviews your responses and produces a legally binding will that accurately reflects your wishes. The document is stored securely online — accessible to the right people when they need it, protected by encryption from everyone else.

What You Can Do with an Online Will

A well-designed will writing service handles everything a traditional solicitor would, in a fraction of the time.

Appoint an executor. The person you trust to carry out your wishes — a partner, sibling, or close friend — named clearly in the document, with no ambiguity.

Name your beneficiaries. Every asset can be specifically allocated. The family home, savings accounts, personal possessions, jewellery, vehicles — each one directed to exactly who you choose.

Guardianship for children. If you have children under 18, you can formally name who you want to raise them. Without this, a court decides. With it, your wishes are legally binding.

Digital asset planning. Cryptocurrency, online banking, social media accounts, cloud storage, subscription services — your digital life has real value. A modern online will addresses this explicitly.

Funeral and care preferences. Often overlooked, but deeply important for the people you leave behind. Sparing your family the guesswork during their worst days is a profound act of care.

Mirror Wills for Couples: Protecting Each Other

For couples — married or not — mirror wills are the most popular choice, and for good reason. Two complementary wills that mirror each other's wishes mean that if one partner dies, the other is protected automatically. Assets, property, and shared wishes are clearly recorded for both parties, with no ambiguity about who inherits what.

Critically for unmarried couples: without mirror wills (or individual wills with clear beneficiary designations), a surviving partner has no automatic legal entitlement to the estate under the rules of intestacy. Mirror wills close that gap entirely — at a combined cost of £29.99.

What About More Complex Estates?

For straightforward situations — and the vast majority of people's estates are straightforward — a single will or mirror wills will handle everything you need. The point is that there's now an accessible, affordable, legally rigorous option for every situation — not just one expensive option that most people can't justify.

Amendments, Storage, and Peace of Mind

Life changes. A new baby. A divorce. A house move. A death in the family. Your will should change with it. Online services make amendments straightforward — no returning to a solicitor's office, no rescheduling, no additional hourly charge just to update a beneficiary's name.

Secure digital storage means your will is never lost, never damaged in a house fire, and never inaccessible at the moment it matters most. The right people can access it when they need to. No one else can.
