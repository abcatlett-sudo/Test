LAST WILL AND TESTAMENT

OF MARGARET ANNE THORNTON

I, Margaret Anne Thornton, of 14 Oakfield Road, Sheffield, S10 3GH, hereby revoke all former wills and testamentary dispositions previously made by me and declare this to be my last will and testament, made this day in accordance with the Wills Act 1837 (as amended).

1. APPOINTMENT OF EXECUTORS

1.1 I appoint as my Executors:

Robert James Thornton, of 14 Oakfield Road, Sheffield, S10 3GH (my spouse)

Claire Louise Thornton, of 22 Beech Avenue, Leeds, LS6 1PD (my daughter)

1.2 If any Executor is unable or unwilling to act, the remaining Executor or Executors shall act alone. My Executors shall have all the powers conferred on trustees by the Trustee Act 2000 and all other statutory powers.

2. SPECIFIC GIFTS

2.1 I give my engagement ring and pearl necklace to my daughter Claire Louise Thornton, absolutely, if she survives me by 30 days.

2.2 I give the sum of £5,000 to The British Heart Foundation (registered charity number 225971), to be paid from my estate as soon as reasonably practicable after my death.

3. RESIDUARY ESTATE

3.1 I give all my real and personal property not otherwise disposed of by this will (my “Residuary Estate”) to my Executors to hold on trust with power to sell, call in and convert into money all such parts as are not already money.

3.2 Subject to the payment of my debts, funeral and testamentary expenses, I give my Residuary Estate to my spouse Robert James Thornton absolutely, if he survives me by 30 days.

3.3 If my spouse does not survive me by 30 days, I give my Residuary Estate in equal shares to such of my children as survive me by 30 days:

(a) Claire Louise Thornton

(b) Daniel Robert Thornton

3.4 If any child of mine predeceases me leaving children of their own who survive me by 30 days, those grandchildren shall take equally between them the share their parent would otherwise have received.

4. GUARDIANSHIP

4.1 In the event that my spouse does not survive me, I appoint as guardian of any of my children who are under the age of 18 at the time of my death:

Patricia Ann Holloway, of 8 Sycamore Close, York, YO31 8LN (my sister)

5. FUNERAL WISHES

5.1 I request that my body be cremated and that my ashes be scattered at Stanage Edge in the Peak District. I would like a small private service attended by close family only.
